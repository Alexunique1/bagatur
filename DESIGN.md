# Design System: Bagatur BJJ Burgas

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Modern Athletic Heraldry"**

Bagatur translates the supplied warrior crest into a contemporary training academy rather than a fantasy game interface. The approved “Training Hall” composition uses real practice as the focal point: a monumental wordmark and photographic hero move directly into three age-program bands, then open editorial and dark proof sections.

The identity feels forged, disciplined and premium, while documentary photography and calm reading space keep it credible to parents.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Monumental display type and cinematic, human photography.
- Charcoal surfaces, aged metallic warmth and controlled red action.
- Program bands and editorial rows instead of generic card grids.
- Crest geometry used as structure, never as decorative noise.
- Calm confidence before aggression.

## Colors

Forge Black and Hall Charcoal own command surfaces. Antique Gold belongs to identity, achievements and precise rules. Action Red is reserved for trial calls and active states. Bone supports readable, welcoming sections.

**The Red Means Action Rule.** Red belongs to trial calls and active navigation; it is not ambient decoration.

**The Gold Earns Attention Rule.** Gold highlights identity and verified achievement, never large paragraphs.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Oswald with Arial Narrow fallback
**Body Font:** Manrope with Arial fallback

Both families support Bulgarian, Russian and English. Display text is condensed and forceful, not pseudo-medieval. Body text stays modern and calm.

**The One Alphabet Standard.** All three locales receive identical hierarchy and quality.

## Layout

Use full-width image fields, asymmetric editorial compositions and decisive program bands. Alternate dense proof passages with open Bone sections. Desktop navigation is compact; mobile keeps the call, language switch and burger reachable. The schedule becomes day/group lists below tablet widths.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from photography, tonal layering, fine gold rules and occasional hard offset planes. Avoid glassmorphism, neon glow and generic floating shadows.

## Shapes

Forms are crisp, weighty and controlled with very small corner radii. Thin metallic lines and occasional angled crops echo the crest. Avoid repeated shields, medieval frames and decorative runes.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Action Red, light text, phone icon, crisp corners.
- **Secondary:** transparent or Forge Black with Antique Gold border/text.
- **Focus:** clearly visible Bone/Gold outline, never color-only.

### Program Bands

Large photo-backed horizontal or equal-width bands with an age label and direct anchor. They are navigation, not statistic cards.

### Navigation

The full logo is cropped into a compact mark plus wordmark. FAQ is excluded from navigation; Gallery is included. Phone CTA appears before the BG/RU/EN switch. Burger opens an accessible stacked menu.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use `Logo_main.jpg` everywhere the brand appears.
- **Do** let real trainer-and-student photography carry trust.
- **Do** make achievements documented and specific.
- **Do** keep the phone action immediately visible.
- **Do** keep all three locales complete.

### Don't:

- **Don't** reuse the previous site's visual language, imagery or composition.
- **Don't** turn the site into a fantasy-game poster.
- **Don't** use generic rounded icon-card grids, glowing gradients or social-media badges.
- **Don't** fabricate awards, reviews, credentials or academy statistics.
- **Don't** copy Ryan Thomas BJJ's branding.
